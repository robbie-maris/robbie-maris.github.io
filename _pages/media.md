## Selected Policy Impact

### Major policy reforms - T level and V levels

A number of recommendations we made in our report on T levels were implemented by the government.

Includes introducing smaller T levels (**V levels**) and abolishing the T level transition programme.

[Read article](https://feweek.co.uk/v-levels-can-streamline-but-beware-a-re-badging-exercise/)

### Curriculum and Assessment Review

**Curriculum and Assessment Review (2025)**  
My research on English and maths resits was cited in the final Curriculum and Assessment Review in its discussion of effective post-16 English and maths provision.

[Read the review](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/curriculum-and-assessment-review-final-report)

### House of Commons Education Committee

**Further Education and Skills inquiry (2025)**  
My research on T Level access and progression was cited in the House of Commons Education Committee's final report.

[Read the report](https://committees.parliament.uk/publications/49615/documents/264337/default/)

### National Audit Office (NAO)

**Review of T levels**
My research on T levels was used by the NAO in their review of the T level reforms.
[Read the report](https://www.nao.org.uk/reports/investigation-into-introducing-t-levels/)
